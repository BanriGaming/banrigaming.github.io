Craft together

Connect your existing Bancy.gg account in the app, share a copy of a shopping list, and invite friends by their display names. Quantities, completed crafts, and the person who updated each row appear live for everyone on that list.

Local planning works without an account. Shared lists need a website account and internet access. Bank and Blackbox features remain behind BancyCraft access keys and existing website permissions.

Your production ledger, here and there

The Dragonwilds leather and charcoal ledger is built into BancyCraft. You can still use the production ledger on the website.
