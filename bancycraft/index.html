01

Find the right item

Choose your game, then search its catalog. Filter results, see item icons, and add one item or a whole selection to a list.

02

Know what you need

Shopping lists separate vendors, gathering, pre-crafts and target items. Complete a craft to satisfy its inputs, or reset progress when your plans change.

03

Build with friends

Private lists stay on your computer. Share a copy using your Bancy.gg account, invite friends by display name, and see their progress live.

04

Start with a gearset

Browse equipment bundles for each available game and add the pieces you want to your shopping list.

05

Plan your production

The Dragonwilds leather and charcoal ledger lives in the app, with station capacity, merchant orders and profit planning. The website ledger is still available.

06

Keep your workspace

Local planning works without an account. Plans, supplies and private lists stay saved when you close the app or install an update.

03 / GET STARTED

Install once. Keep building.

  1. Download and install. Open BancyCraft-Installer.exe and choose your installation folder.
  2. Choose a game. Browse items or gearsets, then create your first shopping list.
  3. Connect when you want to share. Sign in on Bancy.gg through Settings & updates. Your shared lists appear below private lists in Shopping Lists.
  4. Update inside the app. A fresh startup checks for new versions. Click the corner notification or open Settings & updates to download, then restart to install.
