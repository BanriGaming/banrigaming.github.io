Gaming Journey

Hello, fellow gamers. I am Banri, and my journey into gaming started when I was little, sitting in my brother's room watching him play Homeworld. That small moment opened a door that never really closed.

This website exists because I wanted a place to build useful tools, keep guides close at hand, share collections, and give my friends one clean landing place for the games we keep coming back to.

Away from the screen, I play guitar, enjoy disc golf, take long walks with my wife and our Yorkie, host board game nights, and make as much room as I can for family.
